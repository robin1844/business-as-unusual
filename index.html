      Operating procedure

      • Use Next directive to work through every available directive in a randomly shuffled order.
      • Use Previous to revisit directives seen during your current visit.
      • Select Favourite to save a directive. Select it again, or use the × in Favourites, to remove it.
      • Select Hide this to remove a directive from the main sequence. Restore it at any time from Hidden.
      • Use Submit to propose a workplace one-liner for Robin’s review.

      Records management

      Your favourites and hidden directives are stored in this browser on this device. They will not automatically transfer elsewhere, and clearing browser data may remove them.

      Each directive has a stable internal ID so saved choices remain attached to the correct item when the collection is updated.
